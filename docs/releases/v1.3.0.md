WaterLauncher 1.3 is about the controller and how big picture looks: the D-pad, shoulder buttons and haptics behave, desktop mode works with a controller, big picture is sharp at 4K, Orbit is redone, and many more games get their art.

**Controller**

- L1 / R1 switch between Home, Library and Search from anywhere. Library filters and Orbit's zoom moved to L2 / R2.
- A held D-pad direction keeps repeating even when the stick wobbles at rest, and the stick moves one way at a time instead of stepping diagonally.
- The DualSense touchpad click opens Search, like Create.
- Prompts show the buttons of the controller in use, or keyboard keys while you use the keyboard. You can click them too.
- A press no longer counts twice when Steam's desktop configuration is on.
- Haptics are firmer and consistent: a tick as you move, a bump at the end of a row, a pulse when you choose.
- Desktop mode works with a controller: move between controls with the stick or D-pad, ✕ to press, ○ to go back, L1 / R1 through the sidebar.
- On the keyboard, Esc on Home opens Quick access (with desktop mode), and F11 switches modes.

**Big picture**

- Drawn at your screen's own resolution: sharp at 4K and readable on a laptop. 16:10 and ultrawide screens use the whole screen.
- Orbit shows each game's key art with its logo, puts the game's artwork behind the honeycomb, and opens a game cleanly.
- Backgrounds use full-size screenshots; a game without one gets a soft blurred background instead of a stretched small picture.
- The launch screen stays until the game's own window is up, and WaterLauncher comes back as soon as the game closes, instead of showing the desktop in between.

**Library**

- Many more games get their art and details: folder names with versions and group tags, abbreviations ("GTA V"), brand names ("Marvel's"), editions, games Steam no longer sells, and games only Epic sells (from Epic's catalog). Details and art are fetched once more in the background after updating.
- *Found on this PC* is now *New on this PC*. In big picture, a game matched only by its folder name asks whether it's the right game, lets you pick the right one from the Steam store, or hide it.

**Saves**

- *Settings → Saves* shows whether Syncer is installed, running and connected, and what to do if it isn't. New options: how long to wait for a sync before playing, and whether WaterLauncher may start Syncer.
