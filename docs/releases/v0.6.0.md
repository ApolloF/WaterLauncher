Add-ons: other programs can add information and actions to your games, and run a step before a game starts. The first one is [DLSS Updater](https://github.com/ApolloF/dlssupdater).

**DLSS Updater as an add-on**

- A game's page shows its DLSS version, with Super Resolution, Ray Reconstruction and Frame Generation separately, plus OptiScaler and any anti-cheat.
- If a game update replaced the DLSS files DLSS Updater installed, the page says so. The next time you play, DLSS Updater puts them back before the game starts.
- The page's actions: *Update DLSS*, *Install OptiScaler* (with your DLSS Updater settings), *Restore original DLSS* and *Open DLSS Updater*.
- To connect it: in DLSS Updater, *Settings → General → Connect to WaterLauncher*. Then turn it on in WaterLauncher under *Settings → Add-ons*. This needs [DLSS Updater 1.4.0](https://github.com/ApolloF/dlssupdater/releases/tag/v1.4.0) or newer.

**Add-ons in general**

- *Settings → Add-ons* lists them. Each one is off until you turn it on.
- Turning one on shows what it may do (change files in game folders, download from the internet, read saves), whether its program is signed, and its SHA-256.
- WaterLauncher pins that program. After an update, the add-on stays off until you approve it again.
- Add-ons are separate programs with your normal user rights. They're never elevated and never injected into games.
- Every call has a time limit, and one that keeps crashing is left alone for a while.
- Their before-launch steps appear in the launch sequence and can be skipped. An add-on that fails shows a warning; it never stops the game from starting.
- Other add-ons: pick their `addon.json` with *Add add-on…*. The protocol is in [docs/addon-protocol.md](https://github.com/ApolloF/WaterLauncher/blob/main/docs/addon-protocol.md) (JSON-RPC over stdin/stdout, declarative, so it works in every layout).
