Your whole library: WaterLauncher can now list the games your Steam, GOG and Epic accounts own, installed or not. It's optional and off by default.

**Turning it on**

- *Settings → Library → Your store accounts*.
  - **Steam:** paste your own [Steam Web API key](https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey). WaterLauncher reads your account from Steam on this PC, and the key is stored encrypted for your Windows account. Your *Game details* must be visible in Steam's privacy settings.
  - **GOG:** reads GOG Galaxy's own library database on this PC. Nothing is sent anywhere. This needs GOG Galaxy.
  - **Epic:** *Sign in with Epic…* opens epicgames.com in your browser. After you sign in, paste the code the page shows. WaterLauncher never sees your password, and keeps only Epic's sign-in token, encrypted.
- Then switch on *Show games you own that aren't installed*. In big picture, the same switch is in Settings.

**In the library**

- Owned games show *Owned · Steam* (or GOG, Epic) and appear under *Not installed*. They get covers and details like installed games; installed games are fetched first.
- *Install with Steam* (or GOG Galaxy, Epic) opens the store to install the game. Once it's installed, the next scan merges it with the owned entry and keeps your favorite, hidden state and title.
- Steam and GOG playtime is shown for games you haven't installed here. Epic doesn't provide it.
- Owned games update a little after WaterLauncher starts and twice a day, or with *Update owned games now*.
- Disconnecting an account removes its owned-only games.

**Under the hood**

- GOG Galaxy's database is read with a small read-only SQLite reader built into WaterLauncher (fuzz-tested, including its write-ahead log). A full SQLite library would have made WaterLauncher several MB larger.
- Store requests only go to Steam's and Epic's API hosts, over HTTPS. A key never appears in logs or error messages.
