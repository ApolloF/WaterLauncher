Big picture mode: WaterLauncher on the TV, driven by a controller.

**Three layouts** (Settings → Big picture, or Settings in big picture itself):

- **Deck** (default): a side rail and rows of games: continue playing, new on this PC, and your library.
- **Console**: one row of games over full-screen art. Press down for the game's details.
- **Orbit**: your games as bubbles in a honeycomb that glides and magnifies around the selected one. L2/R2 zoom out and in.

**Everywhere in big picture**

- ✕ / A plays, △ / Y shows a game's page (favorite, controller mode), ○ / B goes back.
- Options or the PS button opens **Quick access**: controller and battery, haptics, lightbar and sound toggles, a rescan, settings, and the switch back to desktop mode.
- Create / View opens **Search** with an on-screen keyboard (a real keyboard works too).
- **Library** shows every game as a cover grid, with tabs for favorites, recently played and new finds (L1/R1).
- Button prompts follow the controller in use: PlayStation shapes or Xbox letters (can be fixed in settings).
- Haptic ticks as you move, and the DualSense lightbar takes the selected game's colour. Navigation sounds are optional.
- The interface scales to any screen: TV, monitor or laptop.

**Getting there**

- The Big picture button in the sidebar, F11, or the PS / Xbox button (which also brings WaterLauncher to the front from other apps).
- Optional: open big picture automatically when a controller connects, or start in it.

Controllers are read natively through SDL 3.4 (bundled and hash-checked), so input works even when the window isn't focused. Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Esc, Q for quick access, I for details.
